A note on disclosure quality

Indonesian listed golf-operator annual reports prioritise financial statements, ownership structure, board profiles, and segment-level revenue commentary. Operational specifics — grass varieties, irrigation vendor, fertiliser cost, POS system, booking platform, caddy operating structure, cart fleet — are systematically absent from public filings. The richest publicly available disclosure areas are:

  • Total headcount and departmental count (for pure-play operators)
  • Annual round volume and monthly distribution
  • F&B revenue split by outlet
  • Membership accounting (refundable vs transferable, balances)
  • Land area and insurance coverage
  • Tax audit history and corporate-tax position

For investment-grade due diligence on the 'silent' dimensions (agronomy specifics, digital stack, marketing budget, caddy economics, vendor contracts), private-channel diligence — site visit, management interview, vendor confirmation — is required. This site documents what is in the public record so private diligence can focus its scope.
