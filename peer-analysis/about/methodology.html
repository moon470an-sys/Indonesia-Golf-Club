About this analysis

Independent industry research on publicly-listed Indonesian peer operators with golf course assets.

This dashboard summarises company-level data for IDX-listed entities operating golf course assets within Indonesia. The analysis is positioned to support objective industry research — it does NOT incorporate any proprietary, internal, or pre-decisional information. All figures come from publicly available disclosures, are cited at the source-page level where extracted from annual reports, and are timestamped per source.

Inclusion criteria

An entity is included as a Peer if all three conditions are met:

  1. IDX-listed with annual reports filed in Indonesian or bilingual (Indonesian/English) format and indexed in our vector store.
  2. Golf course operations referenced multiple times across the entity's annual report text (≥4 hits in 2+ fiscal years for "CORE" classification; 1–3 hits for "HAS" classification).
  3. Course-level mapping to a real, identifiable golf course in site/data/golf_courses.json (the 149-course curated Indonesia inventory).

21 initially indexed IDX tickers screened down to 13 confirmed peers. Excluded: 4 mining/fuel tickers with no golf operations; 4 property/industrial tickers where annual reports returned 0 golf-keyword hits; 1 borderline ticker (MTLA) whose hits resolved to residential clubhouses and water parks rather than a golf course.

Data sources

  • IDX Annual Reports (FY2022–FY2024) — Primary source. Indexed in a local ChromaDB vector store containing 70,597 chunks across 21 tickers. Queries return passage-level evidence with page citations.
  • Official course websites — for rate cards, course specifications, designer attribution, opening years, and membership pricing (where publicly published).
  • Named industry aggregators:
    • Q-Access — tee-time booking platform (Tier-3 reference)
    • GoGolf — booking aggregator (Tier-3 reference)
    • GolfLux, GolfPass, GolfAsian — review and listing aggregators (Tier-3 cross-reference)
  • Industry associations — Asosiasi Pengelola Lapangan Golf Indonesia (APLGI), Persatuan Golf Indonesia (PGI) — referenced for industry context where publicly disclosed.

Tier methodology

The tier field is a heuristic classification, not an official ranking. It synthesises:

  • Course pedigree (international designer, championship-standard rating, hosted tournaments)
  • Headline green-fee pricing relative to the peer set
  • Membership pricing where disclosed
  • Awards (e.g., ROLEX "Top 1000", HAPA "Best Golf Club SE Asia")

Tiers used: A (premium urban / international flagship), A− (high-quality township anchor), B+ (regional or industrial-estate-affiliated), B, C (not currently used). This is a relative classification within the 13-peer set; not benchmarked to global rankings.

Currency, unit and date conventions

  • Currency: Indonesian Rupiah (IDR) unless explicitly noted (e.g., Trump Lido membership USD 70,000).
  • Unit: Raw IDR values stored as integers. Display formatting compresses to B (billions) or T (trillions) for readability.
  • Fiscal year: Indonesian companies report on calendar year (Jan–Dec). "FY2024" = year ended Dec 31, 2024.
  • USD/IDR reference: Where USD figures appear in source disclosures (e.g., Trump Lido), original USD is preserved; approximate IDR conversion noted at ~Rp 16,200/USD.

Known limitations

  • Operating KPIs not in AR: Annual rounds played, occupancy rate, headcount per hole, F&B capture rate, and average revenue per round are NOT disclosed at the course level by any IDX-listed peer reviewed. These fields are shown as "N/A — Not Disclosed" rather than imputed.
  • Course-segment revenue: Only 6 of 13 peers disclose golf revenue as a separate financial statement segment. Most embed it in "Recreation & Hospitality" or "Other" lines.
  • Membership pricing transparency: Only 2 of 13 peers (SMRA Gading Raya, DILD Graha Famili) publish a full tiered membership schedule. Most quote "contact for details" and pricing for Trump Lido is sourced from press coverage (Tier-3).
  • AR text extraction quality: BKSL annual reports had degraded text extraction (scanned/protected PDFs). Profile fields for BKSL are conservative; cross-referencing against course-level public sources was used to populate the dashboard where possible.
  • Bundled vs net rates: Some courses publish bundled rate cards (cart + caddy + tax included) while others publish a base green fee with extras priced separately. The pricing comparison page indicates publisher / bundle structure but does not normalise to a single net-GF basis. Apple-to-apple comparison requires reviewing the notes column on detail pages.

Reproducibility

All data files behind this dashboard are JSON files in data/, generated from intermediate CSVs. The pipeline:

  1. 21 IDX annual reports downloaded → parsed to text via pdfplumber.
  2. Text chunks embedded with multilingual-E5-small (ChromaDB persistent collection).
  3. Per-ticker golf-relevance scan via vector queries with strong golf keywords.
  4. Per-peer profile / financial / pricing / membership / operating-signal evidence extraction.
  5. Cross-reference with curated golf_courses.json for course-level facts and rate cards.
  6. JSON output with banned-token audit (no internal/codename leakage) before publication.

Update cadence

Annual report data is updated as new fiscal-year reports are filed (typically Q1–Q2 of the following year). Pricing data is re-verified on a quarterly basis or as triggered by significant rate-card changes. The footer of the Overview page reflects the most recent generation timestamp.
